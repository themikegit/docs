invoicR is available in English and Serbian (Latin script). Use the language switch at the top of the sidebar to change it. invoicR remembers your choice in this browser for a year.

The app language changes:

- Menus, forms, messages, and the sign-in screens
- How dates and numbers appear in the app

The app language doesn't change your invoices. Each invoice has its own **Invoice language**, which sets the language of its PDF. See [Invoice PDFs](/app/invoice-pdf).

The KPO book is always in Serbian.
