Every invoice has a PDF version. The invoice page shows a preview, and **Download PDF** saves the file as `Invoice Oct07-2026.pdf`.

## What's on the PDF

- Your logo, if the company has one
- The invoice number, issue date, and trading date
- **From:** your company details, including tax ID and registration number
- **Billed to:** the client's details
- Payment details: your bank account (for domestic clients), IBAN, and SWIFT
- Items with quantity, rate, and line total
- Subtotal, tax per rate, total, and the amount due
- Your company's invoice note at the bottom

Cancelled invoices are marked as cancelled on the PDF.

## Invoice language

The **Invoice language** you choose on the invoice sets the PDF language. It doesn't depend on the app language.

| | English | Serbian |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title | INVOICE | FAKTURA |
| Dates | `31 Oct, 2026` | `31.10.2026.` |
| Amounts | `$ 3000.00` | `3.000,00 USD` |
| Tax ID label | VAT | PIB |
| Registration no. label | ID | MB |

<Tip>
  An invoice with no tax still shows a tax line at 0%, as paper invoices do.
</Tip>
