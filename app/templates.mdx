An item template stores an item's name and description. Use templates for work you invoice regularly, such as a monthly retainer.

Templates don't store the amount, price, or tax rate. You enter those on each invoice.

## Save a template

1. On a new invoice, type the item's name and description.
2. Open the **Templates** menu next to the item and click **+ Save item as template**.

The item needs a name before you can save it.

## Use a template

Open the **Templates** menu next to an item and pick a template. invoicR fills in the item's name and description.

## Delete a template

Open the **Templates** menu and delete the template. Invoices that already used it don't change.
