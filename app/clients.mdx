Clients are the buyers on your invoices. Each client stores contact details plus two defaults for new invoices: currency and invoice language.

## Add a client

Go to **Clients** and click **New client**. Fill in the form and click **Create client**.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Name** | Yes | Shown on invoices and in the client filter |
| **Email** | No | |
| **Tax ID** | No | Printed on the invoice |
| **Registration no.** | No | Printed on the invoice |
| **Currency** | Yes | `EUR`, `RSD`, or `USD`. Defaults to `EUR` |
| **Invoice language** | Yes | English or Serbian |
| **Street**, **City**, **State**, **ZIP / Postal code**, **Country** | No | Used for the address on the invoice |

When you select a client on a new invoice, invoicR fills in the client's currency and invoice language. You can still change both on that invoice.

## Addresses

invoicR formats the second address line by local convention:

- **United States:** city, then state and ZIP. For example, `Mashpee, MA 02649, USA`.
- **Everywhere else:** postal code and city. For example, `21000 Novi Sad, Srbija`.

A client with the country set to Serbia (or left empty) counts as domestic. Domestic clients see your local **Bank account** on the invoice. IBAN and SWIFT show for every client.

## Edit or delete a client

Open the client from the **Clients** list. Edits don't change invoices you already issued, because each invoice keeps a copy of the client's details.

You can't delete a client that has invoices.
