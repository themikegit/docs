A company is the seller on your invoices. You can have several companies, but exactly one is **Active** at a time. The active company issues every new invoice, and the invoice list and KPO export show its invoices.

## Add a company

Go to **My companies** and click **New company**. Enter a **Business name** or **Your name** (at least one is required). The business name appears as the sender on invoices.

The form has these sections:

| Section | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| **Business** | Logo, business name, your name, tax ID, registration no., activity code |
| **Address** | Street, city, state, ZIP / postal code, country |
| **Contact and payment** | Email, phone, website, bank account, IBAN, SWIFT |
| **Invoice note** | Text printed at the bottom of every invoice, for example your activity code or a VAT exemption |

invoicR removes spaces from the IBAN and converts the IBAN and SWIFT to uppercase when you save.

### Logo

Click **Upload logo** and choose a PNG, JPG, WebP, or SVG file up to 2 MB. The logo appears at the top of your invoice PDFs.

## Switch the active company

Open a company and turn on **Active**, then click **Save changes**. The previously active company becomes inactive. To stop issuing from the active company, make another company active instead.

## How changes affect issued invoices

invoicR copies your company details into each invoice when you create it. Editing a company later, including its logo, doesn't change invoices you already issued.

## Delete a company

You can delete a company only if:

- It isn't the active company. Make another company active first.
- It has no invoices.
