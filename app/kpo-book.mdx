The KPO book (Knjiga o ostvarenom prometu paušalno oporezovanih obveznika) lists your income for a calendar year. invoicR builds it from your invoices for the active company.

## Export the book

1. Go to **Invoices**.
2. If you have invoices in more than one year, pick the year next to **Export KPO**.
3. Click **Export KPO**.

invoicR downloads a PDF named after the year and your business, for example `KPO 2026 Studio.pdf`.

## What's included

- **Header:** your tax ID (PIB), name, business, address, and activity code from the active company.
- **One row per invoice**, sorted by trading date. Each row shows the trading date, the invoice number, and the client.
- **Amounts in RSD.** invoicR converts each invoice's subtotal (before tax) using the NBS middle rate on its trading date. For foreign-currency invoices, the row also shows the original amount and the rate used.
- **Totals** at the bottom.

All income goes in the **Prihod od delatnosti od izvršenih usluga** (services) column. The products column is always 0.

Cancelled invoices aren't income, so the book leaves them out. The book uses the trading date, not the issue date, to decide which year an invoice belongs to.

<Warning>
  The export needs exchange rates. If the rate service is unreachable, you see "Exchange rates couldn't be loaded. Try again in a moment."
</Warning>
