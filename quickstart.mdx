This guide takes you from a new account to a downloaded invoice PDF.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open invoicR and sign up or sign in. Every page except sign-in and sign-up requires an account, and you only ever see your own data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your company">
    Go to **My companies** and click **New company**. Enter at least a **Business name** or **Your name**. Add your tax ID, address, and payment details so they appear on your invoices.

    Your first company becomes active automatically. The active company issues all new invoices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a client">
    Go to **Clients** and click **New client**. Enter a **Name**, and choose the client's **Currency** and **Invoice language**. New invoices for this client use these as defaults.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the invoice">
    Go to **Invoices** and click **New invoice**. Select the client, check the dates, and add at least one item with an amount and a price. The summary on the right updates as you type.

    Click **Create invoice**. invoicR saves the invoice and opens it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the PDF">
    On the invoice page, click **Download PDF**. When you send the invoice, set its status to **Sent**. When the client pays, set it to **Paid**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  You can't edit an invoice after you create it. Only its status can change. Double-check the client, dates, and items before you click **Create invoice**.
</Warning>
