invoicR is a web app for issuing invoices as a Serbian entrepreneur. You keep your companies and clients in one place, create invoices in English or Serbian, download them as PDFs, and export your KPO book at the end of the year.

## What you can do

- **Issue invoices** in EUR, RSD, or USD with automatic numbering such as `Oct07/2026`.
- **Download PDFs** with your logo, bank details, and an invoice note.
- **Export the KPO book** (Obrazac KPO) for any year, converted to RSD at the NBS middle rate.
- **Watch turnover limits** for bookkeeping (6,000,000 RSD per calendar year) and VAT (8,000,000 RSD in any 365 days).
- **Work in English or Serbian**, both in the app and on the invoices you send.
